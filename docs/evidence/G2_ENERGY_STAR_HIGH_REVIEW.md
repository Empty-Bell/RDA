## How to review one row

1. Open the PDP link and verify the product/SKU, its ENERGY STAR presentation,
   and the specs shown for that exact SKU.
2. In the hosted artifact, compare `pf/page-*.json` field `energyStarFlg`,
   `next/sku-*.html` field `energyStarFlag`, and `bridge/sku-*.json` ENERGY
   STAR rows recorded for that SKU. Their files and SHA-256 hashes are attached
   to the corresponding record in `energy-star-assessment.json`.
3. Re-run the Current Model Index capture when a fresh EPA state is needed.
   A result can change when EPA updates its current list; the HIGH outcome here
   is limited to the source snapshot captured in run 35547213219.
